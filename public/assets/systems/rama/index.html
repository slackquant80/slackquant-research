Strategy architecture

01 · Regime

TIP 1/3/6/12-month mean momentum. Positive → offensive; otherwise the stronger of IEF and BIL (ties to BIL).

02 · Selection

Rank Global10, take top six. No separate positive-momentum screen.

03 · Allocation

36 monthly returns, 50% diagonal shrinkage, long-only minimum variance, 25% offensive cap. Initial warm-up uses equal weights.

Accounting contract

USD adjusted-close proxy. Completed common month-end signal, next common trading-day close execution. Prior portfolio earns through execution close; fee is deducted multiplicatively at the execution close; the new target earns from the next close-to-close interval. 10 bp per side (full replacement 20 bp). One-way turnover = 0.5 × L1; fee = 0.002 × one-way turnover. Current calendar MTD/YTD and open holding return remain separate.

Daily-first operating statistics: completed daily net marks, actual elapsed-year CAGR, 252-session annualized volatility and zero-rate Sharpe. The final completed mark excludes the next open-period entry fee. No actual trade or live account record.

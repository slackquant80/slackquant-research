Strategy Architecture

Signal Ensemble

  • Diversified broad-market risk signals assess changing market conditions.
  • Medium- and long-horizon momentum signals are evaluated alongside volatility and correlation measures.
  • A short-horizon dual-momentum component is designed to capture accelerating trends.

Portfolio Construction & Risk Management

  • Complementary momentum sub-strategies are combined within an ensemble allocation framework.
  • A slow-moving allocation overlay helps reduce sensitivity to short-lived market reversals.
  • The USD/KRW hedge ratio adjusts dynamically based on a standardized USD/KRW signal.
